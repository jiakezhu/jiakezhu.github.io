先给旧版留一个位置

更新个人网站的时候，我想到：既然这里要记录成长，网站本身的成长也应该留下来。

旧版把我的教育、工作、AI 项目和生活兴趣放在一张长页面里。罗马斗兽场前的照片、深色背景、金色文字，还有“多学科、多语言、多行业”这些关键词，组成了那个阶段的自我介绍。它承载过真实的努力，也陪我整理过自己正在走的方向。

所以，在新的页面替换它之前，我想给它留一个可以回去看的位置。

保留一个能走进去的过去

浏览保存的旧版网站 ↗

上面的图片是一张真实的首页截图。点击入口，可以继续浏览旧版的教育、工作经历、作品与生活部分，也可以使用它原来的语言按钮和交互。这样，读者看到的既是那一版网站的样子，也包括当时内容之间的关系。

保存的版本对应 GitHub 上 2026 年 8 月 26 日的提交，保存日期是 2026 年 10 月 4 日。这两个日期代表不同的事情：一个是版本来源，一个是我把它放进历史记录的时间。保存时，这份首页与公开网站的首页内容一致。

查看对应的 GitHub 版本

旧版的页面和本地图片一起保留，外部链接仍然指向原来的目的地。字体、地图等部分外部服务仍然需要网络；它是一份保留原貌的历史页面，并不意味着互联网上所有关联服务都被保存了下来。

从一份档案，走向一个人的故事

这次改版，起点是一段口述的成长故事。从宁波咸祥的小村庄，到杭州、成都和巴黎，很多重要的选择都围绕着同一个愿望：走出去，看看更大的世界。

语言一直在这条路上。高中毕业后，我开始教英语，也一直保留着教学的热情；大学选择法语，在巴黎又学了一年西班牙语。后来，AI 和云计算成为新的工作与实践方向。网站需要容得下这些不同的身份，也需要让它们之间的联系变得清楚。

首页，把重点重新摆好

首页保留固定的 Jiake ZHU 标识，把“多学科、多语言、多行业”放到右侧，用学校、企业与语言元素提供具体线索。姓名与关键词之间，头衔逐个自动切换：腾讯云销售、AI Builder、英语老师、语言学习者、跨文化连接者、世界探索者，再到 Always Day One 和 Pragmatic Romantism 两句短短的提醒。

每个头衔有自己的颜色和小图标，导航与语言切换也重新调整，让不同语言的内容能够落在相近的布局里。联系方式写出名称，微信入口同时放入公众号，减少寻找入口的成本。右上角把音乐和明暗模式放在一起，Sunflower 使用完整音频循环播放。开场时尝试默认开启；如果浏览器需要先有一次互动，音乐就从第一次点击或触摸开始。进入首页后，右上角会提示播放或关闭；即使之前关闭过音乐，再次进入也能看到开启入口，并记住读者的选择。

这一轮也向旧版重新学习排版：章节大标题和标签居中，中文用宋体，其他语言用衬线字，给标题与正文留出呼吸的空间。桌面章节导航合为一行，语言按钮写成 EN、CN、FR、ES，保留国旗。章节滚入屏幕时，标题字符短暂跳动，再逐步还原成正常文字；数字动态增长，卡片浮入，照片轻微移动，让阅读保留一点探索的感觉。

这一轮也恢复了旧版“我不只用 AI，也把它做成真正能用的东西”的作品集标题，修正长章节滚动时的导航高亮，并统一所有联系方式的按钮样式。

统计数字也换成更清楚的无衬线字，保留滚动增长的动效。手机端的经历 Logo 和名称固定在同一行，长名称在自己的文字区域换行。首次访问默认英文，之后记住读者选择的语言。开场现在从第一屏开始，首页在它结束后才出现，减少加载时的突然跳转。

Story，让文字和漫画一起讲

打开书本的动画，成了进入 Story 的一段过渡。成长漫画用连续的画面讲述人生阶段，旁白跟随网页语言切换；其中最困难的外貌与心理阶段，也按照我描述的经历呈现。

日志部分继续收录技术实践与生活记录。此前的十一篇文章补充了更完整的正文，文章内部也准备了中、英、法、西四个版本。选择一种语言后，从列表点进文章，可以继续用同一种语言阅读标题、正文和目录，而不只是在外层换一个标签。

Journey，让地点可以被阅读

旅途部分改成一张可以探索的地图，分成世界、中国和欧洲三个视角。地图保留 65 个已有地点，求学路线连接宁波、杭州、成都与巴黎；这条线表达求学经历，并不代表每一次旅行的先后顺序。

现在，选择任何一个地点，都能读到简短的介绍，了解它的地理与文化特色。城市、山岳、村庄和区域会按各自的性质描述。宁波、杭州、成都、巴黎，以及几处有照片的旅行站点，还保留我的个人记忆。当地介绍与个人经历放在相邻但清楚的段落里，四种语言都能阅读，也有进一步了解当地的资料入口。

留下版本，也留下继续生长的空间

回看改版前的网站 ↗

这次也把公开旧版的页面和图片保存下来。读者可以从日志重新走进去，比较两版不同的表达方式。历史版本只在打开入口后加载，让当下的首页继续保持轻一些。

新版页面、旧版归档和这篇日志，都保存在个人网站的同一份版本历史里。这是一次可以回看、可以继续修改的记录。我希望以后每次更新，都能让网站更接近真实的生活：既写出做了什么，也慢慢说明为什么要做。

A place for the earlier version

While updating my personal website, I realised that a place which records growth should also preserve some of its own growth.

The earlier site gathered my education, work, AI projects and interests into one long page. A photograph in front of the Colosseum, a dark background, gold lettering and the words “multidisciplinary, multilingual, multi-industry” formed my introduction at that stage. The page carried real effort and helped me organise the directions I was exploring. Before replacing it, I wanted to leave somewhere that visitors could return to.

A past that can still be explored

The image above is an actual screenshot of the earlier homepage. The archive lets visitors continue through its education, experience, projects and life sections, using the original language buttons and interactions. This preserves both the appearance and the relationships between the things I chose to put on the page.

The saved version comes from the GitHub commit dated 26 August 2026. I archived it on 4 October 2026. Those dates describe different things: the source version and the day I added it to this history. At the time of capture, its homepage matched the publicly available homepage.

The original pages and local images are kept together. External links still lead to their original destinations, and services such as fonts and maps may require an internet connection. Preserving this page does not preserve every external service connected to it.

From a profile to a personal story

This redesign began with an oral account of growing up. From a small village in Xianxiang, Ningbo, to Hangzhou, Chengdu and Paris, many important decisions revolved around the same wish: to go beyond home and see a larger world.

Languages have always belonged to that path. I began teaching English after high school and have kept my enthusiasm for teaching. I chose French at university and studied Spanish for a year in Paris. AI and cloud computing later became new directions for work and practice. The website needs space for these different identities and for the connections between them.

Giving the homepage a clearer focus

The homepage keeps the fixed Jiake ZHU signature. The three themes—multidisciplinary, multilingual and multi-industry—sit on the right, supported by university, company and language elements. Between the name and those themes, titles appear one at a time: Tencent Cloud Sales, AI Builder, English Teacher, Language Learner, Cross-cultural Connector and World Explorer. Always Day One and Pragmatic Romantism add two short personal reminders.

Each title has its own colour and small icon. Navigation and language controls have been adjusted so that different languages occupy a similar layout. Contact methods display their names, and the WeChat entry includes my public account. Music sits beside the theme control at the top right. The full Sunflower track loops, with playback attempted during the opening animation. If the browser needs an interaction first, music starts with the first click or tap. A notice on arrival offers playback or a way to turn it off. Visitors who previously turned it off still see an invitation to play, while the site remembers their choice.

This round also returns to the earlier site’s layout: centred chapter headings and labels, serif type and more breathing room between titles and text. Desktop chapter navigation occupies one row. Language buttons read EN, CN, FR and ES, with their flags retained. As a chapter enters the screen, its title briefly cycles through characters before resolving into the words. Numbers count up, cards rise into view and photographs move gently with scrolling, giving the page a sense of exploration.

This round also restores the earlier portfolio heading, fixes navigation highlighting through long sections, and gives every contact method the same button style.

The statistics now use clearer sans-serif numbers while keeping the scrolling count animation. On phones, experience logos stay beside the company name, which wraps within its own column. First-time visits default to English, then remember the reader’s language choice. The opening sequence starts on the first screen and reveals the homepage when it ends, avoiding a sudden jump during loading.

Letting words and pictures tell the story

The opening book animation provides a transition into Story. A growth comic follows the stages of my life, with narration that follows the selected website language. The difficult period involving my appearance and confidence is also represented according to the experiences I described.

The journal continues to hold technical practice and everyday reflections. The previous eleven articles have fuller bodies, with Chinese, English, French and Spanish versions inside each article. Selecting a language now carries through from the list to the title, body and contents, rather than changing only the outer labels.

Making places readable

Journey is now an atlas with world, China and Europe views. It keeps the existing 65 stops. A study route links Ningbo, Hangzhou, Chengdu and Paris; it describes my education, without claiming to show the order of every trip.

Every selected place now opens a brief geographic or cultural introduction. Cities, mountains, villages and regions are described for what they are. Ningbo, Hangzhou, Chengdu, Paris and several photographed stops also retain personal memories. The local introduction and my experience sit in distinct neighbouring paragraphs, available in all four languages, with a link for further reading about the place.

Keeping versions and leaving room to grow

Return to the earlier website ↗

The earlier public pages and images have been saved too. Readers can return through the journal and compare how the two versions tell the story. The archive loads when its entry is opened, keeping its resources away from the current homepage’s initial load.

The redesigned pages, the earlier archive and this journal share the same website version history. This is a dated record that can be revisited and revised. I hope each later update brings the site closer to the life it describes: what I do, and gradually, why I choose to do it.

De un perfil a una historia personal

La portada conserva la firma fija Jiake ZHU. Esta etapa también recupera el título anterior del portfolio, corrige la navegación activa en secciones largas y unifica los botones de contacto.

Hacer que los lugares se puedan leer

Journey es ahora un mapa con vistas del mundo, China y Europa. Conserva las 65 paradas existentes. Una línea conecta Ningbo, Hangzhou, Chengdu y París para representar mis estudios, sin afirmar que muestra el orden de todos mis viajes.

Cada lugar seleccionado abre una breve introducción geográfica o cultural. Ciudades, montañas, pueblos y regiones se describen según su naturaleza. Ningbo, Hangzhou, Chengdu, París y varias paradas fotografiadas conservan también recuerdos personales. La presentación local y mi experiencia aparecen en párrafos vecinos distintos, disponibles en los cuatro idiomas, con un enlace para conocer más sobre el lugar.
