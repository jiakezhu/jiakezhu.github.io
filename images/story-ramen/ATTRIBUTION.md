# Scene provenance

The complete Ramen-Shop scene, compressed models, baked textures, matcaps, screen videos and seven sound samples are by Jesse Zhou.

- Repository: https://github.com/enderh3art/Ramen-Shop
- Original experience: https://www.jesse-zhou.com/
- Original source paths are retained below this directory.
- Retrieved from the public main branch on 2026-10-28, for the requested local My Story adaptation.

The source architecture and baked environment art are retained. The original name lettering is replaced at runtime, personal screen UVs are normalized, and the original vending-menu, project-detail and introduction textures are adapted for Jiake Zhu’s own content. The original geometry and artwork remain in the source files. The upstream package declares `UNLICENSED`; these assets are not covered by the adapter’s Three.js MIT dependency license.

Three.js and its official loaders and decoders are distributed separately in assets/vendor and assets/story-vendor.
