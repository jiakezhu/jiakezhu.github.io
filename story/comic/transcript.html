The First Journeys

I grew up in a coastal village in Xianxiang, Ningbo.

As a child, I often stayed in my dad's truck and travelled with him on deliveries.

That was my first glimpse of the world beyond home.

A World Beyond Home

Visits to relatives at Chinese New Year were my chance to see city life.

My sister would take me out to play. Those are some of my warmest childhood memories.

I came to believe that studying hard was my way out.

A good university could open up more possibilities.

03 / 16

Running, Jumping, Playing

In fourth grade, I represented Xianxiang and won the Yinzhou district high jump competition.

I also came fourth in the 800 metres.

I kept training. In junior high, I often won the school's 400- and 800-metre races.

I started basketball in third grade. By sixth grade, I could do a between-the-legs layup.

The Door I Almost Reached

My grades were uneven in my first two years of junior high.

In my final year, I caught up.

I qualified for a top provincial high school's special admissions exam.

But I fell short of the minimum score.

05 / 16

Back to the Desk

That setback hit me hard. Its shadow stayed with me for years.

Under enormous pressure, I returned to preparing for the regular high school entrance exam.

In the end, I finished first in my class.

I entered Zhengshi High School in Ningbo, still some way from what I had hoped for.

06 / 16

Under Other People's Gaze

As I grew through high school, my underbite became severe.

I was given hurtful nicknames and mocked.

I became painfully aware of how others saw me. My confidence faded.

Being around people gradually became stressful.

07 / 16

A Light in the Night

Some nights, I hid in the dormitory bathroom and cried.

I woke early, stayed up late, and poured much of my time into studying.

In my last two years of high school, I often ranked first in my year. English had always come naturally to me.

08 / 16

Learning and Teaching

My university entrance exam did not go as well as I had hoped.

I chose Zhejiang Gongshang University over a Project 211 university outside Zhejiang.

I began teaching English after high school. I have kept teaching ever since.

I loved languages and trusted my ability to learn them, so I chose to major in French.

University gave me more freedom, but the distress over my jaw remained.

09 / 16

My Family Beside Me

My family took me to Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital for a consultation.

I decided to have corrective jaw surgery.

Before surgery, I needed orthodontic treatment.

It lasted more than two years, much longer than I had expected.

10 / 16

After the Long Wait

During treatment, my underbite became more pronounced, and the emotional strain grew.

I withdrew from social life and focused on studying. I consistently ranked first in my major, academically and overall.

In the second semester of my junior year, I finally had surgery.

11 / 16

A Brighter Chapter

Surgery changed my appearance. I began to regain the confidence to face everyday life.

Two months later, I secured recommended admission to the School of International Business at Southwestern University of Finance and Economics.

I also received the National Scholarship. After so much pressure and waiting, it was a bright moment in my life.

12 / 16

Into the Working World

After securing my postgraduate place, I interned at Mingle Advisors, a Finnish firm in Hangzhou.

After graduation, I worked in French-speaking overseas sales at Market Union in Ningbo, helping close nearly US$1 million in orders.

Then I moved to Chengdu for graduate school.

Chengdu was a happy chapter. I studied, interned at Alliance Française, and continued teaching English.

13 / 16

Choosing to Go

Then came another choice: should I study abroad?

Going would mean delaying graduation.

I thought about it for a long time. Since childhood, I had wanted to see the world.

I chose a one-year exchange at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University.

14 / 16

A New Direction

Before leaving, I spent four months in overseas talent recruitment at ByteDance in Chengdu.

I helped ByteDance's Seed AI team recruit top talent in North America.

That experience drew me into AI and pushed me to learn more.

With a new direction taking shape, I left for Paris.

The Limits of My World

During my year in Paris, I also spent a year learning Spanish.

I have always loved languages. I explored Paris, France, and other parts of Europe.

The limits of my language are the limits of my world.

Still Exploring

After returning from Paris, I joined Tencent Cloud as a direct sales intern.
